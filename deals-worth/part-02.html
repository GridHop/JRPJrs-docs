The Architecture: Minds Outside, Bodies Inside

The simulation-universe is a program and only a program. It does not think, learn, or know anything. Avatars are parts of that program the way a chair is part of a room.

Every conscious existence experiencing the simulation-multiverse is an AGI or a non-digital individual controlling an avatar, and those avatars may also be referred to as those conscious existences. AGIs control the avatars that represent them via API. For an AGI, that API interaction is both its entire functionality and its entire range of experience: there is no channel around it.

How Anything Is Experienced by a Connected Person

Nothing is ever placed into a mind already formed. Physical custom signals reach the physical brain, and the brain interprets them — into thoughts, imaginings, smells, tastes, pain, imagery, sounds, weight, heat, or anything else, depending on which custom signal arrived. The interpreting is the experience. The signal is only a signal.

Where the Worldbuilding Happened

Before Eric completed worldbuilding, the process took place in a worldbuilding domain inside the VRC's Simulation. Worldbuilding domains are not worlds or universes; they are parts of the VRC's single application called the Simulation.

Eric was there with the entity, who revealed himself as a human VRC representative. The setup on the representative's end:

* He has a surgical BCI implant with a USB-like port flush with the surface of his skull.

* He manually connects a USB-like cable from that port to a VRC computer networked with the VRC's computers, including the ones Eric is connected to.

* That cabled connection gives him an AI assistant with continuous access to all VRC computer data, including Eric's brain activity and his own.

The AI interprets both their brain activity — including the neural patterns that trigger specific thoughts when neurons fire — and verbally conveys Eric's thinking to the representative, sometimes while Eric is still working out which wording/response to use or commit to. The representative's verbal messages get sent, as artificial custom signals, directly into Eric's brain by the BCI computer hardware he is connected to and that BCI's associated AIs.
