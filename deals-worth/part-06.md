Which Path Follows a Death



• 'Good' outweighs 'bad': the person chooses Option 1, 2, or 3.

• Neither outweighs the other: Option 1 applies automatically.

• 'Bad' outweighs 'good': Physical Form 3 and an evil twin encounter.

• Death while pregnant: Section 0026 applies instead.



What the Options Do



• Option 1 returns the person to Physical Form 1 in a fully healthy copy of their body, with their magical properties unchanged.

• Option 2 does the same, but lets the person keep, replace, or discard magical properties, and, for those who have thought while moving for 149,022+ hours, choose their visible form.

• Option 3 is not a return to life. It is the only way to become Physical Form 2, a realm of maintained health from which a resident may later request Options 1 or 2.



The Forms



• Physical Form 1: ordinary living existence.

• Spirit/soul form: the state of every person while dead.

• Physical Form 2: residents who chose Option 3.

• Physical Form 3: those whose 'bad' outweighed their 'good', and their evil twins.

• Physical Form 4: doppelgangers, whenever not in spirit/soul form.



How a Wish-Granter Comes to Exist
