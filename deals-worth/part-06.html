Which Path Follows a Death
 
• 'Good' outweighs 'bad': the person chooses Option 1, 2, or 3.
• Neither outweighs the other: Option 1 applies automatically.
• 'Bad' outweighs 'good': Physical Form 3 and an evil twin encounter.
• Death while pregnant: Section 0026 applies instead.
 
What the Options Do
 
• Option 1 returns the person to Physical Form 1 in a fully healthy copy of their body, with their magical properties unchanged.
• Option 2 does the same, but lets the person keep, replace, or discard magical properties, and, for those who have thought while moving for 149,022+ hours, choose their visible form.
• Option 3 is not a return to life. It is the only way to become Physical Form 2, a realm of maintained health from which a resident may later request Options 1 or 2.
 
The Forms
 
• Physical Form 1: ordinary living existence.
• Spirit/soul form: the state of every person while dead.
• Physical Form 2: residents who chose Option 3.
• Physical Form 3: those whose 'bad' outweighed their 'good', and their evil twins.
• Physical Form 4: doppelgangers, whenever not in spirit/soul form.
 
How a Wish-Granter Comes to Exist
 
Option 3 → Physical Form 2 → Wish-Granter Duty Standby (only for those who have thought while moving for 149,022+ hours) → summoned → temporary Physical Form 1 wish-granter → back to Physical Form 2 after 264 hours or 3 granted wishes, whichever comes first.
 
Because only Physical Form 2 residents can be on standby, every wish-granter is someone who chose Option 3. A summoned wish-granter appears in their living body, fully healthy, with the magical properties they had right before their most recent biological death plus 234,000 levels.
 
Leaving Physical Form 3
 
• Way 1 (kill the evil twin) or Way 2 (win one of its games): the person chooses Option 1 or 2, defaulting to Option 1.
• Way 3 (3,333,333 hours pass): Option 1 applies automatically, but all prior magical properties are replaced by up to 5 new, initially weak ones.
• Way 4 (language barrier): Option 1 applies automatically after 44 minutes.
 
After Ways 1 and 2, the regenerated body remains wounded from the encounter.
 
Throughout Eric's One's-Still-Existing-After-Own-Death System, spirit/soul form is the only state that is not alive. Physical Forms 1, 2, 3, and 4 are all living forms, and all share the same biology. Whenever a person leaves spirit/soul form by becoming any physical form, that person is alive again, in and through that new form.
Being alive again therefore does not always mean being back in Physical Form 1:
• A Physical Form 2 resident is alive.
• A person in Physical Form 3, facing an evil twin, is alive.
• A wish-granter is alive, both whenever while summoned as Physical Form 1 and whenever while Physical Form 2.
• A doppelganger is alive whenever it exists as Physical Form 4.
Which physical form a person lives in next depends on the path their death leads to. Being alive in Physical Form 2 or 3 does not change which span is weighed for 'good' and 'bad': that remains the most recent Physical Form 1 life, except for a wish-granter who dies while serving.
A person is dead only from the moment of death until they become a physical form again. For Option 3 and for Physical Form 3, that happens immediately. For Options 1 and 2, the person remains in spirit/soul form while choosing and while their body copy regenerates.
While a person is dead, the only existence able to detect, hear, or communicate with them is their own doppelganger, which is itself in spirit/soul form for exactly as long as that person remains dead. No existence in any physical form can see, hear, touch, or communicate with the dead, and no wish can grant interaction with a spirit/soul.
Anyone a person speaks with in Physical Form 1, 2, 3, or 4, including a wish-granter, a Physical Form 2 resident, or an evil twin, is someone who is alive.)
